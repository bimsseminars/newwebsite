BIMS‑1 Understanding Injection Molding of Thermoplastics

This seminar focuses on understanding the phenomena behind injection molding and their influence on part quality, shrinkage, warpage and performance.

Audience

Design engineers, mold makers, molders, project leaders, analysts and material specialists.

Program Highlights

  • Polymer thermal and rheological behavior
  • Effect of pressure on polymer properties
  • Flow process and geometry effects
  • PVT and crystallinity concepts
  • Packing phase optimization
  • Shrinkage development
  • Warpage mechanisms
  • Fiber-filled materials
  • Residual stresses
